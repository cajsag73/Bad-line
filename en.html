Soundboard for calls you don't want

Call Jammer

Tap one or more sounds to mix them. Tap again to turn that sound off. Hold the phone with the call close to the speaker.

Fake a dropped call

Tap an ending: everything breaks up, cuts out dead, then that ending plays. Hang up right after.

How to make it convincing

  • Close to the mic. Hold the phone with the call 10–20 cm from the speaker, with the microphone (bottom edge) facing the sound.
  • iPhone: set Mic Mode to Standard in Control Center during the call, or a lot gets filtered out.
  • Dropped mobile calls usually just go silent. The tones come from the old landline network and work best if you say you're on a landline.
  • Say a few words yourself. "Hello? I can't hear you…" over the noise sells it.
